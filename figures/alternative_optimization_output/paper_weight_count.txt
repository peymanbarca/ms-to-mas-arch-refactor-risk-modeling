With a 0.1 simplex grid and strictly positive weights, the three-objective weighted-sum search evaluates 36 weight vectors per benchmark/runtime-condition group. Across 3 benchmarks and 8 runtime conditions per benchmark, this yields 864 weighted-sum selection instances (one selected architecture per weight vector and group), alongside 72 lexicographic selection instances for the three priority policies. These are selection instances; the number of distinct architecture candidates selected may be lower because different vectors/policies can select the same candidate.
